Once incorporated, this section will give the company name, legal form, share capital, registered office, company registration number, EU VAT number and the name of the publishing director.

Contact

For any question, including about your data, write to us through the Request a demo form (in French). A dedicated postal address and e-mail address will be given here with the company details.

This version is a demonstration: it runs on the computer of the person presenting it and is not hosted by a provider. The name, address and phone number of the host will be published here when the service goes live.

Demo data

The companies, people, vehicles, number plates, rates and figures shown in the app are fictitious. Any resemblance to an existing company or person is coincidental.

The figure “≈ €200k per year, per business unit” on the home page is an estimate by the founders, based on their experience. It is not a measurement.

Personal data

The Request a demo form stores what you enter (name, company, contact details, message) in the demonstration database. This information is only used to reply to you. It is neither sold nor passed on to third parties.

You can ask to see it, correct it or have it deleted through the same form. The data controller and the retention period will be specified when the company is incorporated.

Cookies

The site uses no advertising and no audience measurement. It only sets cookies needed for it to work: the login session, the chosen interface style (theme and light or dark mode) and the state of the navigation bar. They do not require consent.

The photographs, illustrations and videos on the site were generated by artificial intelligence. They show no real brand, company or person.

Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap and its contributors (ODbL licence). The fonts are free Google Fonts, served by the site itself.
